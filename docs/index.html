01 / OUR STORY

From “hey”
to always.

It started at the gym. And pretty quickly, we couldn’t get enough of each other.

Our first date had all the makings of a very us kind of adventure: a summer road trip with no A/C, dinner at Abuelo’s in Bricktown, a walk, and hours parked outside Sonic, talking, laughing, and getting to know each other over ice cream.

Since then, it’s been the little things that mean the most: the silliness, the laughter, the affection, and the way we push each other to be better. When we moved into our first place together, Brandon asked, “Will you marry me?” Hope said, “Yeah.” Just like that, our next chapter began.

In September 2022, we said hello and goodbye to our baby boy. Forever part of our family, forever held in our hearts.
