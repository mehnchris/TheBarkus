01 / OUR STORY

From “Hey”
to Always 💍❤️

It all started with a simple “Hey.”

And then… somehow, one gym encounter turned into us realizing we just couldn’t get enough of each other. 😂❤️

Our first date was the perfect introduction to the kind of adventure we’d have together: a summer road trip with no A/C, dinner at Abuelo’s in Bricktown, a walk around town, and somehow ending up parked outside Sonic for hours—talking, laughing, eating ice cream, and getting to know each other.

And honestly? We’ve been talking, laughing, and making memories ever since.

Over the years, it’s been the little things that have meant the most—the silliness, the inside jokes, the affection, the late-night conversations, and the way we constantly push each other to dream bigger, laugh harder, and become better versions of ourselves.

Then came our first place together.

Somewhere between unpacking boxes and building a life, Brandon asked, “Will you marry me?”

Hope said, “Yeah.”

No hesitation. No long speech. Just yeah. ❤️

And just like that, our next chapter began.

Our story has also carried moments of incredible love and unimaginable heartache. In September 2022, we said hello and goodbye to our baby boy. Though our time with him was far too short, he will forever be part of our family and forever held in our hearts.

Today, we celebrate not only where we started, but everything that brought us here—the laughter, the adventures, the hard days, the beautiful days, and all the little moments in between.

From “Hey” to forever…

This is us. And this is always. ❤️
